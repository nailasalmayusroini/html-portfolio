The Best Movies According to Naila

My top 3 movies of all time

Epic

This movie is an animated movie with some action and some romance mixed together. I think what makes this movie so meaningful is that it captures the kind of fairytale that I've always loved and with the right atmosphere. I loved how there are a lot of different forest creatures and the queen of the forest has the power to control the whole forest. It also captures the romance of strangers to lovers that lasts even after all the action happened, not to mention a loyal captain enforcer that loved the queen deeply.

Flipped

This movie is such a wholesome about your first love kind of movie. It really captures the innocent love and the characteristics of a child crush growing deeper.

The Greatest Showman

This movie shines the audience with the message of self love. This movie shows different kinds of people with different physiques battle with the judgement in other people's eyes, finding their true self worth in the process and loving it whole heartedly.
